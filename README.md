# Next Prayer

**Your mosque's prayer times, live in the Mac menu bar.**

Next Prayer is a tiny macOS menu bar app that shows the next prayer and a live countdown — `Asr in 02:14` — right next to your clock. And not an estimate from an astronomical formula: it uses the exact timetable your mosque publishes on [mawaqit.net](https://mawaqit.net), the network trusted by 8,000+ mosques worldwide. When your mosque says Isha is at 22:47, your Mac says 22:47.

## Features

- **Always in sight** — the next prayer and countdown live in the menu bar, no window to keep open
- **Your mosque's real times** — search any city or mosque name, pick yours, done in ten seconds
- **The whole day, one click away** — Fajr, Sunrise, Dhuhr, Asr, Maghrib, Isha
- **Friday-aware** — Jumua times appear on Fridays, all by themselves
- **A gentle heads-up** — optional reminder 10 minutes before each prayer
- **Hard to miss when it counts** — the countdown blinks softly in the final 10 minutes
- **Discreet mode** — show only the countdown, keep the rest to yourself
- **Never sleeps on you** — after Isha, it quietly rolls over to tomorrow's Fajr

## Requirements

- macOS 14 (Sonoma) or later
- Your mosque listed on [mawaqit.net](https://mawaqit.net) — if it isn't yet, ask your mosque to join; it's free for them too

## Privacy

The App Store label reads **"Data Not Collected"** — our favorite sentence. No account, no ads, no analytics, no third-party SDKs. The app talks to exactly one place: mawaqit.net, to fetch your mosque's timetable. Full policy: [Privacy Policy](https://bilalkhettab.github.io/NextPrayer/).

## Support & FAQ

**My mosque doesn't show up in search.** It needs to be on the mawaqit.net network. Search by city as well as by name — mosque names are sometimes registered differently than you'd expect.

**The times differ from my other prayer app.** That's the point. Most apps *calculate* times with astronomical methods; Next Prayer shows the timetable your mosque actually follows. When they disagree, Next Prayer matches your imam.

**The menu bar says "Prayer —".** Usually a hiccup reaching mawaqit.net. Click the panel and hit Refresh; if it persists, [open an issue](../../issues).

Anything else: [open an issue](../../issues) or email [bilal.khettab@gmail.com](mailto:bilal.khettab@gmail.com). Feature ideas are very welcome — this app grows by request.
